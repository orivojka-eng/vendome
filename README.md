## 📖 The Story Behind Vendôme

Rooted in the timeless prestige of Paris’s historic Place Vendôme, **VENDÔME** merges century-old high jewelry craftsmanship with an ultra-responsive, modern digital boutique. Designed for discerning collectors and connoisseurs, the maison showcases bespoke creations adorned with rare Colombian emeralds, Ceylon sapphires, and ethically sourced conflict-free diamonds set in 18k Fairmined gold.

The digital storefront captures the luminous radiance of precious stones through delicate typography, glassmorphism overlays, and fluid e-commerce interactions.

---

## ✨ Key Functions & Experience

### 1. Responsive 3-Column Luxury Navigation
- **Adaptive Screen Layout**: Tailored viewports for mobile, tablet, and 4K displays with zero layout shift.
- **Full-Screen Atelier Drawer**: Fluid slide-out navigation for mobile clients featuring curated collections and concierge booking.
- **Global Currency Switcher**: Seamless toggles between EUR (€), USD ($), and GBP (£).

### 2. High Jewelry Catalog & Precision Filters
- **Gemstone Classification**: Filter by Diamond, Colombian Emerald, Royal Sapphire, and Burmese Ruby.
- **Precious Alloy Selection**: Toggle between 18k Yellow Gold, Platinum 950, and Rose Gold.
- **Dynamic Sort Architecture**: Re-order pieces by rarity, novelty, and price.

### 3. Interactive Shopping Bag & Checkout Flow
- **Slide-Out Concierge Bag**: Real-time quantity adjustments, price calculations, and tax computations.
- **Complimentary Services Included**: Insured courier delivery, bespoke ring sizing, and certificate of authenticity.
- **Simulated Secure Checkout**: Smooth transitions into order confirmation.

### 4. Bespoke Client Services
- **Private Consultation Scheduler**: Dedicated modal to book one-on-one appointments at the Paris Salon or via virtual video call.
- **Interactive Wishlist**: Live client wishlist counter with persistent local state.
- **Heritage & Archival Drawer**: Explore the maison's 100-year history of royal commissions and high jewelry exhibitions.

